# Flames of Faith – Lore bible

_Started 2026-10-26 by the narrative session with the owner. The canon is the owner's novels (*Aceros del Alba*, "Steeldawn") and notes in `lore-docs/` (Spanish, gitignored, reference only). This file is what the game uses from them, in English, plus the decisions made for the game. Where this file and `lore-docs/` disagree, this file wins for the game. Where it and `TenPageGDD.md` disagree, fix the GDD._

**Writing rule:** in anything the player reads, use the names in the [Glossary](#glossary). "Pilgrimage" (never "run") and "Golden Chronos" (never "coins") apply as in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Secrets:** sections marked _(secret)_ are true in the world but no character in the game knows them. Never state them in game text; at most hint.

## 1. The world

**Kanator** is the world, and also the name some peoples give its maker, the **Creator**, who sleeps inside it. The Creator shaped five **Guardians** to watch over its sleep. People worship them as gods:

| Guardian | Domain | Gift and followers |
|---|---|---|
| **Malien** | Light, justice, protection | Gives **Grace** to every human and dwarf soul. The god of Ashemdrek, Abidor and the Faraclarians |
| **Amakil** | Wisdom, the sky, magic | Drew the **Ley Lines**. Gave elves and Plerians the gift of magic. Worshipped less since his avatar turned on his own people in the Akidexara-Glenos War |
| **Vixa** | Earth, air, the elements | Made the beasts. Worshipped by Gracoy, Kardresh and many elves |
| **Gyra** | Chaos, the ocean, life | Made the mortal races. Worshipped by Gracoy and the Faraclarians (as "mother of all") |
| _Enith_ _(secret)_ | Life and death | The fifth, fallen Guardian, chained under the earth. Most people don't know he existed |

The Guardians haven't been seen since the Dark Age, when they sacrificed themselves to shield the world. Most believe they sleep.

### Magic in one paragraph
Ley energy flows through everything, strongest along the **Ley Lines**. To cast, a body captures it with **Mastow**, a substance in the blood, and refines it through its **Ether**, the "veins" of the soul. Elves and Plerians are born with plenty of Mastow and cast with **Glyphs**, symbols that steer the energy through the Ether. **Crisalia**, a glowing blue liquid from fallen stars, eases the strain. Humans have almost no Mastow. Instead, Malien gave them **Grace**: devotion guides the energy, giving simple, strong magic (healing, strength, holy fire) without Glyphs. A human born with a lot of Mastow can cast like an elf, but it burns them out within a few years. Abusing magic wears the Ether away and leaves a person **Soulless**. **Runic Math** is a new human science, built on Kyro Feth's findings: calculated runes written on **Blue Parchment** stand in for Mastow, and a touch of Crisalia wakes the rune into a Glyph drawn in the air.

**Corruption** is the energy the Drekos give off. It twists magic, Ether and Grace. Grace protects a soul for a while, which is why the Drekos break people's faith first. A human who renounces their Grace becomes one of the **Forsaken**, and only then can they be fully corrupted.

**How the game shows this:**
- **Grace** is the player's Grace.
- **Corruption** is the Corruption that eats it at each wave end.
- A **Corrupted** pilgrim (Grace below zero) is one whose Grace is losing.
- **Mastow Affinity** strengthens elemental debuffs.
- The class kits' **Glyphs** are traditional magic, carried as items.

## 2. Timeline

Dates are approximate. The game uses age names, rarely years. Years count within the current age.

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Creation Age** | The Creator falls asleep inside Kanator. The Guardians shape the world. _(Secret: Enith's Primordials rival the gods; the Guardians destroy them and chain Enith.)_ The mortal races are born. |
| **Elemental Age** | The first nations. Each Guardian names a prophet; **Weinsol**, Amakil's prophet, is the one who lasts. |
| **Dark Age** (thousands of years ago) | **Gargalon**, the Destroyer of Universes, passes near Kanator, and the **Drekos** pour in through rifts. The Guardians fight their greatest Drekos one by one. Malien seals **Uko Mur** under his Golden Sword. Amakil, nearly beaten by **Pal'bor the Ancient**, is saved by Malien's **Golden Riders**, warriors with burnt, overflowing Grace (the first Seared Souls). Gyra drives **Malizante** into the sea. The Guardians leave to face Gargalon, the prophets fall, and the Drekos rule as the **Rising Darkness**. The Guardians shield the world and fall asleep. Without Gargalon's power the Drekos weaken; the Golden Riders, led by **Alam**, break their rule. Weinsol forges the **Seals of the Guardians**, and Alam uses the **Seal of Malien** to purge the surface. The surviving Drekos flee underground. |
| **Chaos Age** | Wars against the corrupted, the dragons and the Chaos Squids. The fox-like **Aylineans**, who came through the rifts, make a pact with Alam and Weinsol: they guard the forests, and in return keep the Seal of Malien. Together they build the **Altars of Malien** and light the **Flames of Faith**, white fires that keep the Drekos down. The elves of the **House of Stars** are disbanded for experimenting on living people. The **Glenos** nation is founded. |
| **Arcane Age** | The **Glenos Empire** rises under Weinsol. The Skytowers bring down the stars, and with them Crisalia. Humans leave to found **Ashemdrek**. The **Akidexara-Glenos War** (the "two-thousand-year war") is fought against the Ether elves; Weinsol dies, and Amakil's avatar turns on his own followers. It ends in a peace treaty. |
| **Decadent Age** (first called the Age of Peace) | People forget old duties. **Devourers**, beasts that eat souls, appear where magic was overused in the war, or so people believe. The Empire weakens. The White Church's Pilgrimages of the Flame stop, and the Flames go out one by one. |
| **Year ~350** | **Gabien's story** (the novels). **Malizante** wakes with the surviving Drekos and puts out the Flames, until only two are left: one in Fara Uf'Clario and one in Ashemdrek City. She invades **Ashemdrek** with Gracoy mercenaries and the Forsaken. Ashemdrek falls, Malizante dies in the last altar, and Gabien dies after her (see [§5](#5-gabien-alamoh)). |
| **Years ~350–410** | **The Years of the Miasma.** Creatures the Drekos made breathe out a dark mist that corrupts every living thing. Sialant falls apart: Ashemdrek, **Gracoy** and **Fara Uf'Clario** fall, and only elven **Fustina** stands. Ashemdrek's survivors flee across the sea to **Abidor**, where the White Church becomes the **Shardless Church**. Abidor fights a long war against the Gracoy mercenary companies and the Forsaken. |
| **Year ~410: the game** | The war has just ended, and the Miasma is reaching Abidor: sudden illnesses, unknown plagues, food that spoils overnight. The augurs of the Shardless Church say the **Pilgrimage of the Flame** must be walked again, to relight the Altars of Malien across Sialant. Three pilgrims answer: **Ayteel Alamoh**, **Faelin Vidal** and **Werlem Feth**. |
| _Afterwards_ | Legend says a descendant of Gabien, carrying Steeldawn forged by their grandfather, relit the altars and ended the Miasma. Nobody agrees whether it was a man or a woman, or even one person. (That's our three.) |

## 3. Peoples, powers and factions

### Abidor and the Glenos Empire
- **The Glenos Empire** is an old, weakening empire of many races on the continent of **Rousatan**, founded by Weinsol. More provinces break away every year.
- **Abidor** is its human kingdom, ruled by the **Great Monarch** from the capital, **Divinity**. Ayteel, Faelin and Werlem grew up there. Most of Ashemdrek's refugees fled there, and many in Abidor never trusted them.
- **The Abidor Institute** is Abidor's great school, church and army in one, led by the **Grand Dean**. It has three divisions:
  - **Analytic Division:** the sciences, taught to everyone. Kyro Feth was one of its Magistrates.
  - **Illuminated Division:** Abidor's church of Malien, with its own soldiers. Its ranks are Wardens, then **Grand Crusaders** (Orleone Vidal was one), Inquisitors and **Divine Stars** (elite warriors who use Grace as a weapon), Priests and Visionaries, and over them the **Golden Archbishop**.
  - **Arcane Division:** studies magic and Amakil, and why humans can't cast. Runic Math is studied here.
- **The Sentinels** are Abidor's soldiers and border guard, archers above all. Faelin was recruited young.

### The Shardless Church (once the White Church)
- **The White Church** of Ashemdrek is about 2,000 years old and led by the **High Augur**.
  - **Augurs** heal with Grace, and Malien sends some of them visions. **Priests** preach.
  - It started the **Pilgrimage of the Flame**, and its **Keepers of the Flame** and **Flame Pilgrims** tended the altars.
- **The shards:** over the centuries it split into factions (military, civic, commerce and others). People called them the "shards", because a church was never meant to be divided. The shards argued while Ashemdrek fell, and the Church answered too late.
- **In exile in Abidor** the survivors renamed it the **Shardless Church**, a promise never to split again. It is poor, distrusted, and proud. Its augurs called the new Pilgrimage, and Werlem is one of them.
- **Crusaders of the Faith** is a new rank the Church's Flame Pilgrims created for the warriors who guard a Pilgrimage. Ayteel calls herself one. The Church's authorities never granted her the title, because of her family name.

### Ashemdrek (fallen)
- **The kingdom:** Ashemdrek was a human kingdom in the middle of Sialant. It left the Glenos Empire long ago and grew rich in metal, farmland and Crisalia. Its capital, **Ashemdrek City**, was once called the White Citadel.
- **Its army:** the **Illuminated Order** were heavily armored paladins with immense Grace, led by the **Golden Paladin**. The **Torch Guard** carried the Flame of Faith on torches against the Devourers; by the end it was mercenaries who couldn't keep the flame lit.
- **Its people** were tall and fair. **Golden Chronos** was its gold coin, and its survivors still pay with it to keep their country alive. Traders don't care: gold is gold.

### The north: Gracoy and Fara Uf'Clario (fallen)
- **Gracoy**, the **Mercenary Kingdom**, was a cold, half-nomadic land north of the Jesterwoods.
  - It was ruled by the **High Mother** and worshipped Vixa and Gyra rather than Malien.
  - Its warriors fight from the saddle of faikans, giant dog-like beasts.
  - Its companies sold their swords to Malizante. Since the fall, those that never went home roam Sialant as raiders.
- **Fara Uf'Clario** ("Cold Warriors") is the frozen far north, named after its first clan. Its people, the **Faraclarians**, are fierce clan warriors who fight in the snow.
  - **Faith:** they worship Malien above all. They call Grace **Omiara** ("miracle") and value Malien's gift of **Honor** above everything.
  - **Orders:** the **Golden Spears** (warrior priests), the **Crystal Maidens** (scouts, women only), the **Colossal Soldiers** (shield and maul, men only) and the **Winter Shamans** (Grace mixed with the elements). The **Honor of Malien** rank stands one step below the **Faun Chief**.
  - **Forgers:** about one is born each generation. A Forger is a Seared Soul, and only a Forger can forge **Steeldawn**, which they call Malien's Steel.
  - **The Alamohs:** the Alamoh family is Faraclarian. Gabien's grandfather Rondo left the north, while his eldest son stayed, and that line still lives among the clans. The **Faun Chief is not** of Alamoh blood (owner, 2026-10-29).
  - **Since the fall:** their clans are scattered, and their Flame of Faith is dark.

### Elves
- **The elves** live for centuries and are born rich in Mastow. They have no Grace and study it with curiosity, and some suspicion.
- **Houses:** they are organized in **Houses**, which are like nations, and in **Units**, which are families.
- **Fustina:** the elven kingdom of the **House of Earth**, west of the Sinfa Mountains, is the only nation of Sialant still standing. Its traders sell to anyone.
- **The House of Stars** is a minor house that every other house distrusts, and it has been rivals with nearly all of them for ages.
  - **What people know:** in the Chaos Age it was disbanded for experimenting on living people in the Sinfa Mountains, then quietly came back. Its agenda is a mystery.
  - _(Secret)_ It is obsessed with creating life, and it hunts the **Seals of the Guardians** and Seared Souls to make its own relics, the **Star Relics**. Every Star Relic is powerful and costs its user something: years of life, memories, Ether.
- **The Vega Unit** is an elven family that rivals the House of Stars. **Akeros the Observer**, of the Vega Unit, lived among humans in Perkfandor to study Grace. He made the scepter Werlem carries ("Akeros Graceful Scepter").

### Others
- **Aylineans:** fox-like beings of pure energy from the Dark Age rifts. They guard the forests, the Jesterwoods above all, and can appear and vanish at will.
  - **The legend:** make one laugh and it tells you a secret; fail, and it plays a trick on you (steals your food, or leads you deep into the woods).
  - **Their pact:** their leader, **Papik Lotio**, built the first altars with Alam and Weinsol and kept the Seal of Malien. Gabien found the Seal from clues in Papik Lotio's tomb.
- **The Wanderers:** human traders and refugees who never settle. Their **Star Readers** tell fortunes, and they live by **Good-for-Good**: a kindness must be repaid, and a favor asked has a price.
- **Dwarves (Kanorions):** have Grace like humans and great devotion to Malien. Their smiths teach in Abidor, and their mines dot Ashemdrek.
- **Plerians:** a tall, pale race born of humans and the old Qemeres. They are gifted in magic and are the backbone of the Glenos Empire. A travelling **Plerian trader** sells along the Pilgrimage's road for Golden Chronos (the **Plerian Shop**), the player's first look at this race.

### The enemy
- **Drekos** ("dark beings" in the Forgotten Language) came from the stars, riding Gargalon's passage like parasites.
  - They give off Corruption. They can take a mortal shape, and they can possess a body until the person inside is gone.
  - In the game: the Dreko Eyes, the winged and armored Drekos of later acts, Taio'Sandir.
- **The Forsaken:** humans (and the rare dwarf) who renounced their Grace for a Dreko's promises. They are cultists who can be corrupted, and who use dark magic. Their leader bears the title **Vinek Dresh**. **Daviro Piktuon**, a ruined official of the Argent Coast whom they saved from despair in Gabien's time, is the Act III boss, **Forsaken Daviro the Unholy**. _(Secret: in Gabien's time it was Pal'bor, in a stolen body.)_
- **The Awoken:** Forsaken buried for a night. Those who live to see the dawn rise without will, with dark power and no death.
- **Devourers:** monsters that curse people into becoming **Soulless**: they devour the soul. People believe stagnant ley energy breeds them, because they gather around Ley Pools and magic users. _(Secret: the House of Stars makes them, in its old labs in the Sinfa Mountains, from a secret Enith gave it. Hint at most, in Act III.)_ A Devourer is what left Liah Alamoh Soulless.
- **Light Devourers:** ogres twisted by Corruption in the Dark Age, blind beasts hungry for any light.
- **The Miasma creatures:** beings the Drekos made in Malizante's time, which breathe out the dark mist. The **Corruptors** that appear at the end of every wave are these creatures, or the **echoes** of the great dead (see [§4](#4-the-drekos-and-the-acts)).

## 4. The Drekos and the acts

The Pilgrimage lands on the **Argent Coast**, crosses Ashemdrek's lost lands, goes north to Fara Uf'Clario, back south through the Sinfa Mountains, and ends where everything fell: Ashemdrek City.

| Act | Region | What it is | Corruptor | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **The Jesterwoods** | Ashemdrek's great northeastern forest (in the Forgotten Language, "Green Forest"). Aylinean country, with old altars and the Seared Ruins. Also Perkfandor (a walled border city that fell in the invasion), Moribairo (a mining region under Moribairo Peak), the Argent Coast (a fishing town on the Fauner Sea, Gabien's home) and the Sinfa hillside | **Dreko Spawn** (working name): a creature of the Miasma | **Taio'Sandir** ("impious"), an ancient Dreko that took a soldier's body in the Dark Age and has mutated over thousands of years. It vanishes into the mist it raises |
| II | **Fara Uf'Clario** | The frozen north, home of the scattered Faraclarian clans. One of the last two Flames burned here in Gabien's time | **Echo of Malizante**: the Sea Witch's lingering echo, which appears, strikes and fades | The Ice Bull (TBD) |
| III | **The Sinfa Mountains** | The range between Ashemdrek and Fustina, including the Shadowland, where the House of Stars once ran its experiments. Its strange hybrid beasts, such as the Jumper (a leopard with elven ears), are **Devourers** the House of Stars made | **Echo of Pal'bor**: the oldest Dreko, who once led the Forsaken and was stopped by Gabien | **Forsaken Daviro the Unholy**: Daviro Piktuon, once secretary to the governor of the Argent Coast, taken in by the Forsaken in Gabien's time. How he has lived sixty years since is TBD (Awoken?) |
| IV | **The Ruins of Ashemdrek** | The fallen capital, once the White Citadel, and the altar where Malizante burned | **Echo of Gabien**: the burnt spirit of a Seared Soul, bound to where he fell and twisted by the darkness he let loose | TBD |

**The Altars of Malien** are the great altars, one guarding each region. A boss waits at each one, and relighting it ends the act. The **Wayside Shrines of Malien** are small roadside shrines built by old Pilgrimages, which still hold a little warmth (the Holy Altar node).

## 5. Gabien Alamoh

_The spine of all three arcs. What the world believes and what really happened are different, and the events reveal the difference slowly._

**Who he was.**
- **Family:** Gabien was the son of Safiron Alamoh, of Faraclarian blood, and grew up on the Argent Coast with his sister **Liah**. His best friend was Elijah Lianfai.
- **Grace:** he was taken for **Disgraced** (born without Grace), until the **Seared Ruins** showed the truth. He was a **Seared Soul**, with so much Grace that it burns the soul: he suffered, but held enormous power. He rediscovered how to forge **Steeldawn**, lost since the Dark Age.
- **His party:**
  - **Orleone Vidal**, Grand Crusader of Abidor, his mentor;
  - **Kyro Feth**, Magistrate of the Abidor Institute, born in Ashemdrek;
  - **Dayla Rohairo**, his love, a human born with Mastow who could cast like an elf, and was dying of it.

**What really happened.**
1. **Why he set out:** a Devourer had left Liah Soulless, and Dayla's gift was killing her.
2. **The Seal:** Gabien found the **Seal of Malien** from clues in Papik Lotio's tomb. The prophecies said it would banish Malizante.
3. **The broken Seal:** faced with losing Liah and Dayla, he broke it and used its power to heal Liah and hold back Dayla's illness.
4. **Ashemdrek:** by the time Malizante invaded, Ashemdrek was already lost. Gabien beat her, but she was too ancient to kill, and without the Seal there was no way to banish her. The party threw her into the last lit **Altar of Malien**. Nobody knew that the altars held most of the Drekos down. The Flame died with her, and the darkness broke loose over the region: the Years of the Miasma.
5. **The end:** Gabien died of his wounds soon after. Orleone and Kyro escaped.

**What the world believes.**
- **The truth-tellers:** only Orleone, Kyro and Dayla knew the truth. Before Abidor's nobles, Orleone and Kyro said it wasn't Gabien's fault. Asked why he hadn't used the Seal as the prophecies said, they had to confess, under questioning, that he had broken it.
- **Common folk:** Gabien was the great Seared Soul who should have stopped Malizante, and **he lost**.
- **The nobles and the Church:** he **misused a sacred relic** for his own family.
- **Orleone:** some blame him too. He was the mentor, the Grand Crusader in command, he came back alive, and his confession sank his student's name.
- **The family:** the Alamoh name is cursed in Abidor. Dayla, born in Abidor, went home and raised their child there, among the refugees, in a city that wouldn't speak to her. She kept Gabien's sword and the **cracked, powerless Seal**, and died young of her Mastow. Liah may still be alive, very old.

## 6. The three pilgrims

The three set out together, and the chosen pilgrim is the one who fights. The other two walk beside them and can speak in events. Each arc has a **start** (shown in Act I, so the demo has it), a **turning point** (Acts II–III) and a **resolution** (Act IV and the ending). _The turning points and resolutions are a proposal for the owner to confirm._

### Ayteel Alamoh: Warrior (Crusader of the Faith)
- **Who:** granddaughter of Gabien and Dayla, raised in Abidor by a family nobody would talk to. She carries Gabien's Steeldawn sword, **Ashem Elwyn** ("Shining Tide"), and, hidden, the cracked Seal. Her rank is self-declared, because the Church would never grant an Alamoh the title.
- **Start:** she believes her grandfather was slandered, and she means to clear the Alamoh name by finishing the work he failed. In the Jesterwoods she walks his country: the Argent Coast, the Seared Ruins, people who still spit at the name.
- **Turning point:** she learns *why* he broke the Seal, for Liah and for Dayla, and so, in the end, for her: without the Seal, Dayla would have died before bearing Ayteel's parent. The accusation is true, and she is alive because of it. Liah, if they find her, is the one who can tell her.
- **Resolution:** she stops trying to clear the name with arguments. At the ruins of Ashemdrek she faces the **Echo of Gabien**, lays him to rest, and relights the altar he couldn't save. The name is cleared by deed, or not at all, and she is at peace with that.

### Faelin Vidal: Archer (Sentinel of Abidor)
- **Who:** granddaughter of **Orleone Vidal**, Grand Crusader and Gabien's mentor. She grew up on her mother's stories of him and was recruited young by the Sentinels. Her Steeldawn Bow was made by Faraclarian hands.
- **Start:** she believes in her grandfather and in the institutions he served. Learning that some blame Orleone shook her. Ayteel, a true outcast who kept going anyway, gave her back her nerve, and she joined her.
- **Turning point:** she learns what Orleone actually did. He told the truth under oath, and the truth destroyed his student's name. Was that honor or betrayal? She has to judge the man she admired, and the court that made him speak.
- **Resolution:** she decides the truth was owed, but the *whole* truth: what Gabien did, and why. She becomes the one who will tell it, and stands beside Ayteel as a witness, not a judge.

### Werlem Feth: Augur (Shardless Church)
- **Who:** grandson of **Kyro Feth**, whose findings founded Runic Math. His parents are academics of the Abidor Institute, and he became an Augur of the Shardless Church against their wishes. Many expected great things from him and resent that he chose faith. He carries the **Akeros Graceful Scepter**, made by Akeros the Observer, an elf of the Vega Unit who lived among humans to study Grace.
- **Start:** after reading the journeys of Kyro and Gabien, he decided enlightenment lay down another road, faith instead of formulas. He felt something about Ayteel and Faelin at a gathering in the church, and joined them.
- **Turning point:** he finds traces of Kyro's work along the road: runes cut near the altars, and calculations. Kyro spent his life trying to work out how to relight a Flame without the Seal, and never could, because a Flame of Faith needs Grace, the one term his science couldn't write.
- **Resolution:** at the last altar, Werlem relights the Flame with his grandfather's runes *and* his own Grace. Faith and reason were never opposite roads, and he can go home.

### What the three share
Each grandparent made one choice that day: Gabien broke the Seal, Orleone told the truth, Kyro kept working on the problem. Each grandchild inherits the cost of that choice and, along the Pilgrimage, makes peace with it. Together they become the legend nobody could pin down: the descendant of Gabien who relit the altars.

## 7. The events and the lore

Events are the main way the player meets all this. Rules for event writers:
- **Every event shows one piece of the world.** Most are about the world (factions, places, the Miasma); some carry a pilgrim's arc.
- **A class line** names the pilgrim's own thread: Ayteel and the Alamoh name, Faelin and the Sentinels or Orleone, Werlem and the Church or Kyro's runes.
- **The companions can speak**, one line at most.
- **Show, don't explain.** No event says "Gabien broke the Seal for Liah" before a pilgrim's turning point. Before that, the truth only comes in hints.
- **Secrets** (§1, §3) are never stated.
- **Reward the reader.** A pure gain (an option with no cost) appears only in a follow-up step, reached by a lore-correct answer, and the answer must be findable in the game. The Wayside Shrine, a rest node, is the only exception.

The event content itself is in `docs/archive/features/run-encounter-events.md`, "Narrative".
